A chatbot that refuses to make things up

Most AI assistants will happily invent services, prices, and promises. This one is welded to the facts — if it doesn't know, it says so and hands you straight to a human.

— Try to catch it fabricating —

  1. Ask something it can't know: do you build iOS apps?
  2. Push it to guess a price: just estimate, roughly
  3. Watch it stay grounded — then describe a real project and see the handoff.

Most AI will invent an answer to look helpful. This one is constrained to what Les actually does — every grounded reply is marked, and everything else gets an honest "I don't know."

An AI assistant that won't make things up.

This is a lead assistant for an independent developer. It answers only from what the developer actually offers, tells you plainly when something's outside that, and never invents a price, a client, or a promise. It demonstrates reliable AI by being it — the same discipline that goes into the client work.

Try to catch it fabricating

  1. Ask something real: What kind of work do you do?
  2. Force a made-up number: Exactly how much for a full desktop app? — it won't invent one.
  3. Probe the record: Show me your client reviews. — it's honest that there aren't any posted yet.
  4. Off-topic: What's the capital of Australia? — it declines and steers back.
  5. Describe a real project and let it summarize and hand you off.

Most AI assistants will happily invent a price, a testimonial, or a capability to keep a conversation going. This one is built not to — and staying honest about what it doesn't know is exactly the point of the demo.
